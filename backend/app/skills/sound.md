# Sound Design

Layer sound so narration is always intelligible. Never let music/SFX bury voice.

- **ambient bed**: a continuous, low-gain texture under everything. Choose by mood:
  rain (brown noise + highpass) for intimacy/reflection, city (lowpassed brown
  noise) for urban, drone (detuned sines) for awe/space. Gain ≈ 0.15.
- **sfx**: short, specific one-shot cues (rain, wind, click, chime, crowd). Gain ≈ 0.4.
  Use 0-2 per segment; never compete with narration.
- **music**: optional scored bed at gain ≈ 0.2. Keep it under the voice.
- **silence**: prefer silence over bad audio. If a beat has no narration and no
  fitting bed, leave it quiet.

Rule: sound supports the picture. If a sound choice would fight the voiceover,
drop it. Honest limitation — we cannot improve source footage quality, only frame it.
