# Color Grade

Apply one grade per shot for a coherent look. Keep it subtle — over-grading reads
as amateur.

- **clean**: no grade. Neutral, factual footage.
- **nostalgic**: warm lift — slight red boost, slight blue reduction, +saturation.
  For memory, family, history.
- **professional**: cool, neutral, slightly desaturated. For corporate / explainer.
- **cinematic**: teal-orange balance, gentle vignette, soft shallow-DoF blur. The
  default "movie" look.
- **b&w**: desaturated, contrast lift, light vignette. For stark / historical / art.

Rule: pick a grade from the tone of the beat, not at random. Adjacent beats should
share a family (e.g. nostalgic → cinematic) unless the act deliberately shifts.
